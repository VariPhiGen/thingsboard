# Plant shift guide (IST)

## Shifts
- Morning / A shift: 06:00 to 14:00 IST
- Afternoon / B shift: 14:00 to 22:00 IST
- Night / C shift: 22:00 to 06:00 IST (crosses midnight)

## Example operator questions
- What was average power in the morning shift?
- How long did DG SET1 run yesterday night shift?
- Compare today's power vs yesterday.
- Show coolant trend for the last 6 hours.
- Statistics for oil pressure today.
