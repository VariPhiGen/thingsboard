# Fire Suppression Systems Operator SOP

## System Overview
The facility operates two clean-agent gas fire suppression systems to protect mission-critical electrical and UPS infrastructure:
1. **Fire Suppression (Main / UPS Room 1):** 9 clean-agent gas cylinders (FM-200 / Novec), monitored with pressure transmitters, relay gateway, low pressure line, and high pressure line.
2. **Fire Suppression UPS Room 2:** 10 clean-agent gas cylinders safeguarding battery racks and UPS units in UPS Room 2.

## Normal Operating Status
- **Gas Cylinders (Cylinders 1 to 9 / 10):** All pressurized at nominal operating range (typically 25 - 35 bar, or status NORMAL / 0).
- **Relay Gateway:** ONLINE / ACTIVE.
- **Low Pressure Line:** NORMAL (Closed / No leak detected).
- **High Pressure Line:** NORMAL (Closed / No gas discharge).
- **Abort Switch (DI1):** Inactive (Ready).
- **Discharge Pressure Switch (DI2):** Inactive (No release).

## Emergency & Alarm Procedures
1. **Low Pressure Line Alert / Cylinder Drop:**
   - Signifies potential pinhole leak or pressure decay in the manifold or cylinder valve.
   - Do not discharge system. Inspect cylinder pressure gauges and dispatch fire safety technician.
2. **Pre-Alarm (Smoke / Heat Cross-Zoning):**
   - 30-second countdown begins before gas discharge.
   - If false alarm, press and hold Abort Switch (DI1) to prevent discharge.
3. **Discharge Event (High Pressure / DI2 Active):**
   - Immediate total building evacuation of the affected zone.
   - Verify HVAC motorized fire dampers have automatically closed.
   - Do NOT enter room until ventilated and cleared by fire response team.
