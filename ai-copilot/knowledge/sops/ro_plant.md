# RO Plant System Operator SOP

## System Overview
The Reverse Osmosis (RO) Water Treatment Plant supplies purified water for plant utilities, cooling towers, and drinking water.

## Normal Operating Parameters
- **Operating Status:** `FILTERING` during operation, `STANDBY` when storage tanks are full, `BACKWASH` during cleaning.
- **Operating Pressure:** 4.0 – 15.0 psi (typical operating pressure: 5 psi).
- **Water Flow Rate:** 1.0 – 10.0 L/min (normal permeate flow).
- **TDS (Total Dissolved Solids):** Normal permeate < 150 - 250 ppm.
- **Salinity:** Normal < 150 ppm.
- **Electrical Conductivity (EC):** 150 – 300 uS/cm.
- **Water pH:** 6.8 – 7.8 (neutral).
- **Water Temperature:** 20°C – 32°C.

## Operator Checks & Actions
1. **High Permeate TDS (> 175 - 250 ppm):**
   - Inspect RO membrane integrity.
   - Verify antiscalant dosing pump operation and tank level.
   - Check raw water inlet conductivity / feed TDS.
2. **Low Flow (< 1.0 L/min):**
   - Membranes may be fouled or pre-filters clogged.
   - Inspect suction strainer for airlock or choking.
   - Check raw water booster pump and inlet water tank level.
3. **Abnormal Pressure (< 3.0 psi or > 15.0 psi):**
   - Check booster pump discharge pressure and inlet pipeline.
