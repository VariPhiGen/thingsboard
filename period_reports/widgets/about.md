**Periods** &nbsp; Daily, Weekly (7 days), Monthly (calendar month) or Custom (up to 31 days). Days run 00:00-24:00 IST; a period ending today covers up to now.

**Contents**
- **Maintenance outlook** - predictive health, main finding, recommended action, DG service due and last maintenance per equipment
- **DG sets** - run hours, runs, energy, fuel used and refuelled, battery and coolant extremes, daily charts
- **LT panels** - energy, voltage, current, power, frequency and power factor per feeder
- **RO plant** - product-water TDS, EC, temperature (trend indicates membrane wear)
- **Fire suppression** - time at normal pressure, low-pressure days and unstable signals per cylinder
- **Alarms** - summary by type and severity, with the alarm log
- **Maintenance & event history** - logged maintenance work, refuels and predictive warnings
- **Data quality** - data availability and excluded false alarms

**Formats** &nbsp; PDF for review and audit; Excel with one row per day and equipment for analysis.

**Maintenance log** &nbsp; Record every service, repair and inspection on the right. Saving a DG *Preventive service* with *restart the service-due counter* resets the predictive maintenance service countdown.
